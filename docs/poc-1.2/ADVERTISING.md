# Original advertising contracts and spending

The typed career opens the Original advertising screen for its active club
manager. Boards, shirt sponsorship, television rights and the three spending
controls use the existing Core offer, budget and merchandise functions. Native
and typed sessions share command validation and the renewal identity checks.
The screen accepts the explicit numeric reference and canonical crest atlas.

Acceptance retains the selected raw offer and contract bytes. Core still owns
board ordering, bundled boards, sponsor identity, offer consumption and the
group counter. Press/fan spending changes monthly budgets. Merchandise books
cash and its accounting counters, stores the ordered amount and draws the
Source delivery delay. Delivery-pending purchases and stale values are rejected.
Insufficient funds retains the Original handled response, including clearing
the old merchandise amount without inventing a purchase or delivery draw.

World schema **28** saves the optional `economy.advertising_group` byte. Fresh
careers capture the actual initialized Source counter. Projection and capture
retain it, including zero and high-byte values. Older unknown counters remain
unknown through ordinary projections; older schemas cannot silently discard a
known newer counter. This menu requires a known counter rather than guessing
the next bundle identity. Existing sponsor, budget, delivery and account fields
need no new representation.

Expired contracts require a visible presenter. Core checks the sponsor's demand
and computes either the renewal question or sponsor refusal. A confirmed answer
uses the existing renewal operation; a refusal retains the actual mailbox
message. Cancellation, a missing presenter or an externally changed owner
publishes no staged world or RNG effects. The normal SDL entry provides the
Original question/notice presenter. The GUI request, manager/club, revision and
window generation must match before and after presentation and acknowledgement.

Private tests drive the Original offer buttons, spending calculators and notice
acknowledgements in single-English and mixed English/German careers. They compare
the complete world and saved seeds with Core, including the bundle counter,
costs, delivery, sponsor contracts, offer terms and mailbox. Renewal fixtures cover
acceptance, manager decline, sponsor refusal, cancellation, missing presenters
and a presenter that closes the live owner. Synthetic checks cover spending,
stale values, delivery guards and saved-counter compatibility. Screenshots and
matching FBM snapshots remain private.

This does not complete the normal live/calendar turn driver, remaining management
menus, annual transitions or the playable-Beta release gates.
