# National-only annual publication

`national_annual_ready` recognizes the actual July closing boundary with the
completed current national programme, retained Source setup/population receipt,
actual coach/nominee/account owners and no outstanding desktop/event. The
national-only branch of `Session::publish_annual_year` stages one complete
transaction; it creates no club closing or opening desk.

The transaction verifies actual final scores, closes the no-club award ledger
without inventing club prizes, appends the one actual national competition row,
draws the next actual national campaign, advances retained inactive setup fields,
then ages/retires the real origin/free-pool population through shared kernels.
It retains the coach account, nominees, tactical state, personal/shared ledger
and wide player history. No hidden human club/person, club tax, advertising,
contracts, tickets, manager club history or played club standings are created.

The score uses the actual selected coach's cash/deposit, its canonical personal
fund/share holdings and loan ledger, plus the shared Source World-Cup podium
weights for that actual nation. It has a distinct national basis; club-specific
components are inapplicable. National-only scores are deliberately unranked and
never write an Original ranked file. Older known signature words remain saved;
fresh national-only initialization writes its explicit policy0.

Fixed durations finish only after the mandatory final score presentation and
before next-year days. Continuing careers apply actual national daily interest
and nominated-squad training until the first Wednesday, align the actual primary
calendar and publish `inspect_calendar` with its new zero cursor. The frontend
continues seeking a genuine national appointment. Source owner/revision changes
or refused presentations leave the published snapshot and RNG unchanged.

This explicit Extended national-only scope differs from Original's hidden club
selection/person lineage. Whole-Original national-career RNG/ranking equivalence
is not claimed. The model test begins with controlled closing scores; actual
calendar playthrough remains a separate release requirement.
