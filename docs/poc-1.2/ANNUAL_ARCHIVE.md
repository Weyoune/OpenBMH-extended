# Annual title identities and retained history

World schema40 adds optional `annual_title_archive`. A fresh confirmed career
establishes a known empty archive. Older snapshots retain unknown history. Each
retained season contains canonical league/country/competition identities and
actual winner/runner IDs, with the Source title category recorded separately.
The hidden English division's actual internal result remains a league record;
its absent Source award category does not expose a fifth player-facing trophy
column. League Cup results likewise do not invent a native title award.

`stage_annual_title_archive` obtains actual complete league tables, domestic and
European finals, German Masters and the current owned national tournament. The
national facts reader checks Source group/knockout score words; Four-Nations
preserves Source's unknown runner rather than selecting another nation. A World
Cup retains all four actual podium identities. Known suppressed indoor seasons
produce no invented Masters title; an already completed Masters remains retained
even if its display option is later switched off. Missing competition ownership
is an error. The Source disables a country's Super-Cup after a league/main-cup
double. Its absence is accepted only from the owned reigning-title pair; no
self-fixture, replacement opponent or trophy is fabricated.
The helper returns an unpublished archive; only the final atomic annual owner
may install it while advancing the season. Snapshot validation rejects a row
for an unadvanced season, gaps, duplicates and a prefix detached from its
published season count. No archive component alone completes season publication.

Resolved IDs remain stable through club renaming, draw-order shuffles and
promotion. Explicitly imported Original title labels remain separately retained
as exact original-encoding bytes. They are never matched to current club names
or silently removed when a resolved current-season row is appended. Older
unknown history can acquire a new retained range while keeping its earlier
unknown status. Conflicting label imports reject atomically.

Manager career state also retains the optional Source history-window origin word
used beyond fifty seasons. Fresh capture/install preserves its two bytes and
neighbours; ordinary projections preserve old unknown state. The annual history
shift must require that origin when it actually needs it.

Schema40 reserves `regional_playoff:null`; this foundation refuses unknown
nonnull regional state until its real six-date Source owner is installed.
National39 campaign and reserved origin/coach ownership remain intact.

Authored tests cover actual completed table/cup owners, all internal league rows,
strong IDs, codec/snapshot retention, old unknown migration, malformed/duplicate
records, unresolved label bytes and missing real indoor ownership. Independent
Original account/mood/renewal comparisons and bounded actual-v10 account receipts
remain separate evidence. These checks do not claim a played calendar season,
complete annual transaction or Beta readiness.
