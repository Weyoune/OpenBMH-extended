# Gate income, attendance records and prepared-match receipts

World schema 25 retains known attendance history in ManagerMatchState: nineteen
league crowds/prices/catering words, category totals, record values/dates and
explicit global opponent identities. The league income/total-gate and monthly
catering counters keep their existing season-service/economy owners; the new
record does not duplicate them. Original name-pool references are translated at
bounded projection boundaries. Unknown older history stays absent during an
ordinary projection and is not silently initialized to zero.

`book_event_attendance` consumes a saved weather preparation once. It calls the
Core interest, attendance, gate-sharing, catering, season-ticket refund, facility
and income logic with the actual manager data and TextD messages. League size,
round count and home-competition classification are explicit in projected
workspaces; a local token cannot make an English second-division club a German
top-division club. Native paths retain their original lookups and arithmetic.

All affected managers, monthly counters, mailbox messages, records, random
streams and calculated spectator counts stage together. Snapshot SE marks the
booked receipt. A duplicate request, including after save/load, cannot charge or
credit the gate again. SD still denotes weather without a gate receipt; existing
older snapshot schemas remain readable. Older world schemas cannot silently
discard nonempty newer record payloads.

The Session calculation owner can consume a completed receipt into actual league
or domestic-cup score transactions. It uses the stored weather/referee/crowd
values, removes the receipt only on successful publication, and retains saved
next-day services. Mixed league/cup dates still need a combined owner.

Validation includes nonzero/high-word histories, global opponent-name mapping,
old-schema unknown state, malformed references, the 38-round English interest
boundary, actual source-prepared finals, and all twelve first league rounds in
the private four-manager fixture. Calculated crowds, gate/catering effects,
record history, SE save/load, duplicate rejection and actual scoring passed.

These are backend/session calculations. Mode-I [scheduled manager desks](SCHEDULED_DESKS.md)
now precede booked receipts, with SF retaining their phase. Unsolicited offers/coupons,
complete live/result UI, competition premiums and
annual transitions remain release work. Three played seasons are not claimed.
Native single-account money storage is still 32-bit; this record extension does
not solve the separate large-balance overflow issue.
