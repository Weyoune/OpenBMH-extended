# Saved daily progression owner

`finish_initial_event` closes the authenticated last initial manager desk after
pending offers and autosave ownership are resolved. It runs the existing empty
KickOff/play/FinishEvent kernels: lineup/bench status, played flags and marking
references must be updated even when there are no fixtures. Canonical squad/pool
played flags are cleared beyond the bounded human workspace. Free scratch quotes
are released; retained entry40 bidder history remains intact.

The calendar then advances exactly once and records that daily processing is
pending. `advance_clock_step` publishes one authenticated saved phase:

1. Daily finance and Source DayStepRest services.
2. The additional post-event stock tick, when required.
3. Calendar inspection. A quiet date advances into another pending plain-day
   service phase. A scheduled appointment stops for its competition owner.
4. The annual boundary stops for awards and the closing desk before any extra
   post-event market tick.

The clock stores the prior date, season origin, verified epoch, last global
manager, entry kind and phase. Snapshot validation binds it to the saved calendar
cursor, rejects a simultaneous active desk and checks scheduled/annual boundaries.
Presenters cannot use another active manager. Current weekly title relationships
are explicit caller inputs. No human decision or competition result is guessed.

`seek_next_event` stages the complete multi-day search and publishes it under one
revision. A late weekly/monthly failure or changed caller discards all earlier
quiet dates, choices, finance and random consumption. It never seeks past an
already owned scheduled event or annual desk.

Snapshots with progress use **SB**. Existing pre-desktop/initial-desk snapshots
continue writing **SA**, and S4–SA remain readable. The world JSON schema stays21.
Every clock phase roundtrips, including before finance and before the extra stock
tick, so resuming does not repeat those actions. Session-level resume accepts the
saved clock and verifies its epoch against the supplied rules. This does not yet
make the old desktop file-dialog path a clock-status screen.

## Validation and remaining integration

Synthetic tests cover every-phase save continuation, atomic versus stepwise search,
empty-match cleanup, physical scratch ownership, earlier-manager rejection, a late
fifth-week failure, foreign/stale owners, bad epochs, malformed/truncated snapshots
and stopping at real scheduled/annual boundaries.

The actual-data Core oracle compares nine phases from the initial empty kickoff
through weekly/training/interest and both stock entry paths, retaining full human
state, player state, mail, stocks and both random streams. It stops at day4 before
national nomination. The actual four-manager session also reaches day4 with a
save/resume after every phase. Neither comparison plays that national event.

The existing GUI deliberately retains its last-desk guard until the scheduled
competition/desk preparation and presentation owners are connected. National
nomination on day4 is not a quiet date: it must select the real national squad,
retain the national record and handle eligible human coach ownership. Subsequent
fixtures, Toto, manager desks, live matches and annual transitions remain required
before publishing a playable1.2 release.
