# Joining a running shared career

The original desktop's **Neuer Sp.** opens the existing setup and manager
selection screens. One to four human managers are supported. An entire sequence
of new managers is staged and publishes once; cancelling discards it and its
random draws. Existing manager identities and the current desk owner/order stay
intact. German and English clubs use their actual visible domestic leagues;
English tier five remains unavailable.

League and strength controls display the club's existing situation and cannot
reshuffle it. Played results, memberships, competition fields, player ages,
attributes and disciplinary cards remain unchanged. Source automatic lineup
initialization may update the low five card bits, which encode the squad row.
The new manager gets actual Source personal facilities, funds, contracts and
formation defaults; unrecorded AI account history is not manufactured.

OpenBMH deviates from the original: taking over an AI club does not reposition
that club or reroll its players' ages. Existing loans remain binding; the new
human owner's real loan contracts receive Source contract boards. The existing
three outgoing loans per manager / twelve human loan-board slots remain limits.
Pending market rows/bids for the club are cleared as in the original. Entry is
blocked while a pending human transfer offer or national-team role owns the desk,
or after match attendance has already been booked.

Validation includes synthetic takeovers across all nine visible domestic tiers,
loan-owner takeover, rejected repeated/hidden choices, unchanged existing state
and strict save roundtrips. Private GUI runs use actual saved careers for 1→2
and 2→4 managers, including a midseason career with completed matches. Platform
runtime QA is tracked separately; these are native Linux Core/UI tests.
