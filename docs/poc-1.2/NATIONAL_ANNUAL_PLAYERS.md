# National-only annual player lineage

`stage_national_annual_players` visits the actual retained origin slots in Source
league/physical order, then the actual domestic and foreign free pools. A global
player-ID guard prevents double ageing when retirement moves an existing player
to a later pool or club. Source ageing, injury retirement, regeneration, no-list
recruitment scoring and AI line-up kernels retain the real player identities.
The primary-country startup receipt owns pool classification and the actual four
entry40 bidder references used by no-list overflow.

The bounded inactive roster projection operates on real provenance records,
including sparse rosters. It creates no active club, human club/person, fixtures,
financial ledger or padding players. It retains national accounts, old nominees,
source origin IDs/ranks and wide player history. A later actual nomination chooses
its twenty players from the surviving real club population.

Initialized inactive origins in national-only scope may fall below eleven after
actual Source retirement. They have no fixtures and are not padded. Active club
minimum sizes, unknown/uninitialized import minima, roster compactness, identity
and ownership checks remain strict. The original cached retirement-count sentinel
is retained; this change admits its real inactive output rather than changing the
retirement result to satisfy an active-game roster constraint.

The public tests cover both primary countries, normal ageing, a forced real
injury, actual pool/no-list destinations, retained IDs/accounts/nominees,
save continuation and late-owner cancellation. They do not claim a played
calendar season or complete national-only annual publication.
