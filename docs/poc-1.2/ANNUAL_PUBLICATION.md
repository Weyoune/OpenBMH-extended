# Owned annual publication

The club-career transaction is `app/world12_annual_transition`. It accepts only
an exhausted, paid closing season whose real closing desks have committed the
saved `annual_transition` phase. Closing sponsor goals and offers belong to those
desks; this transaction cannot replay them.

It stages actual title facts, Source moods, the next national draw (or the saved
inactive raw-token carry), European qualification, accounts/tax/academy,
remaining auctions, country exchanges, sanctions, cup fields, fixture shuffle,
player and inactive-origin renewal, manager history, archive and owned-manager
highscores in that order. German exchanges require the completed regional
playoff receipt. England exchanges four player-visible groups; the fifth
internal division retains its clubs and simulation role. Source-compatible
named amateur sampling is the defined common-engine correction to FLTD's
undefined-stack newcomer branch.

Tax choices and the actual qualification, promotion, cup, national and score
presentations require callbacks. Absence, cancellation or stale ownership leaves
the published snapshot and random streams unchanged. A ranked score file is
checked against its original bytes and replaced at the final boundary; memory
publication is nonthrowing. Actual canonical IDs and exact unresolved imported
title labels remain retained in the archive.

Fixed durations 1 through 4 decrement after the score update. A duration of 5
remains unlimited. A completed career requires its final score presentation and
stops before next-year day services. A continuing career performs Source's
first-Wednesday services, resets the actual source calendar and Hall/regional
owners, then waits for the separately owned opening desks.

The synthetic coordinator test begins with explicitly authored closing scores.
It checks composition, academy/roster lineage, save continuation and rollback;
it is not evidence of a played calendar season or a release gate. National-only
careers require their distinct real startup and annual population/context path;
this club transaction does not fabricate club accounts or qualification for them.
